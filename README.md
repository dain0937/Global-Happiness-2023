# Global-Happiness-2023

### ☺︎ About the Project Context

- **Objective:**  
  Analyze the factors contributing to happiness disparities across 137 countries and propose data-driven strategies to improve global well-being.

- **Data Source:**  
  [World Happiness Report 2023 (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ajaypalsinghlo/world-happiness-report-2023)

- **Tools Used:**  
  **R Studio**, with packages including `ggplot2`, `dplyr`, and `reshape2`.

---

### 🧹 Data Preparation

- Imported the dataset from a CSV file and renamed columns for clarity.
- Cleaned and transformed the data for analysis.
- Calculated average GDP per capita, social support, and life expectancy for top-ranked countries.

---

### 📊 Exploratory & Statistical Analysis

- **Top 10 Happiest Countries:**  
  Identified and analyzed key well-being factors for the top-ranked nations, including Finland, Denmark, and Iceland.
  
- **Visualizations:**  
  Created histograms, scatter plots, and correlation heatmaps to explore relationships among variables like:
  - GDP per Capita
  - Social Support
  - Life Expectancy
  - Happiness Score

- **Correlation Analysis:**  
  Found strong positive correlations between happiness and:
  - Social Support (r ≈ 0.83)
  - GDP per Capita (r ≈ 0.78)

---

### 🌍 Regional Disparities

- Computed mean happiness and economic indicators by country.
- Highlighted global inequalities and socio-economic contrasts through comparative plots.
  
---

### ⚠️ Outlier Detection

- Applied interquartile range (IQR) methods to identify outliers.
- Example: Afghanistan was identified as an extreme low-scoring outlier despite mid-level GDP factors.

---

### ✅ Conclusion

- Nations with higher GDP, stronger social networks, and longer life expectancy tend to report higher happiness.
- Socio-economic resilience and quality of life play crucial roles in national well-being.
- Regional disparities highlight the need for targeted development strategies.

---

### 💡 Policy Recommendations

1. **Inclusive Economic Growth:**  
   Invest in education, job creation, and entrepreneurship to reduce income gaps.

2. **Strengthen Social Support Systems:**  
   Improve healthcare access, social services, and community resilience.

3. **Transparent Governance:**  
   Combat corruption and enhance public trust through accountable institutions.
