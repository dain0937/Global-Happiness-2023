# Global-Happiness-2023
### **☺︎ About the Project Context:**

- **Objective:** Investigate factors contributing to happiness disparities across 137 countries and propose effective strategies to address these disparities.
- **Data Source:** Utilized data from the **World Happiness Report 2023**, encompassing happiness scores and socio-economic factors.
    
    (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ajaypalsinghlo/world-happiness-report-2023)
    
- **Tools Used:** **R Studio**, including packages such as *ggplot2, dplyr, and reshape2*.
- **Data Preparation:** Loaded and processed the dataset from an Excel file, adjusted column names for usability.
- **Exploratory Analysis:** Explored top 10 happiest countries, computed mean factors to understand their association with happiness.
- **Statistical Analysis:** Conducted summary statistics, histograms, scatter plots, and correlation analysis to gain insights into happiness dynamics.
- **Outlier Detection:** Identified outliers within the dataset to understand exceptional cases warranting further investigation.
- **Conclusion and Recommendations:** Drew conclusions about global well-being dynamics and proposed policy implications and future research directions.
